Drug-pricing curbs hurt Max most, Medanta least

On 9 November 2026 the 30% cap on trade margins for 137 cancer drugs took effect. The Supreme Court reserved its order on 27 October in a petition that seeks margin limits across all drugs and devices sold in hospitals. The score weighs oncology share, government-scheme share, pharmacy distribution share, the modelled EBITDA hit and the valuation premium at risk. Higher means more exposed.
